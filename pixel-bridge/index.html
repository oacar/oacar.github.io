Pixel Bridge support

See the notification details that iOS makes available on a supported Pixel Watch. The connection stays local over Bluetooth.

Private preview
Pixel Bridge is not yet available in either public app store. Compatibility and release plans can change during testing.

What you need

  • The Pixel Bridge companion app on an iPhone
  • The Pixel Bridge watch app on a Pixel Watch
  • Bluetooth enabled on both devices
  • Share System Notifications enabled for the watch in iPhone Bluetooth settings

The current preview has been tested only with an iPhone 17 Pro Max on an iOS 27 beta and a Pixel Watch 4 running Wear OS build CP2A.260603.001.S1. Beta results do not establish compatibility with a stable iOS release. Other models are not yet supported.

How it works

The iPhone app helps the watch find and pair with the iPhone. After pairing, the watch reads notifications from Apple's system-provided Apple Notification Center Service. The iPhone app does not read or relay notification content.

The iPhone app can stay in the background after setup. You can also stop pairing mode without removing the Bluetooth bond.

Setup and troubleshooting

Follow the Pixel Bridge setup guide to pair the apps, grant the required permissions, and recover from a stale connection.

Limits

  • Bluetooth range only; there is no internet or cloud relay
  • Read-only notifications; replies and iPhone dismissal are not supported
  • No missed-notification backfill after an out-of-range period
  • The watch shows only the details that iOS exposes under your preview settings
