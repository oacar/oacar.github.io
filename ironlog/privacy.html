IronLog

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

IronLog does not collect your personal data. Workout data stays on your device and, if iCloud is available, can sync to your private iCloud account. IronLog has no developer-operated server or account system.

Local storage

IronLog stores workout data in its private SwiftData database on your iPhone and stores plate inventory and related calculator settings in local app preferences. Other apps cannot access that sandbox. Use Settings → Delete All Workout Data to remove workout history while retaining your configured library. Deleting IronLog removes its local records and preferences.

Optional iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, CloudKit can sync IronLog data across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer cannot access your private CloudKit data. You can disable iCloud access for IronLog in iPhone Settings. You can manage or delete synced IronLog data from your iCloud account-storage settings.

The hasDonated flag also uses iCloud key-value storage so its thank-you state can persist across reinstalls and your devices. It contains no payment details.

Optional in-app tips

IronLog offers three optional one-time consumable tips. Apple handles payment details through StoreKit, which displays localized prices for your App Store storefront. IronLog processes Apple's verified transaction result to complete a tip, then persists only the hasDonated true-or-false flag. It does not store payment details, receipts, or a transaction history.

For purchase or refund help, contact Apple Support. IronLog does not provide a developer-operated transaction-history or refund system.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

IronLog has no third-party analytics, advertising, social, or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not sell, rent, or share your app data with third parties.

Children's privacy

IronLog is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. It has no account, messaging, or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, its effective date will be updated. Material changes will also be identified in the relevant App Store release notes.
