Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

Setvance does not collect your personal data. Workout data stays on your device and, if iCloud is available, can sync to your private iCloud account. If you opt in, Setvance can also write limited completed-workout summaries to Apple Health. Setvance has no developer-operated server or account system.

Local storage

Setvance stores workout data in its private SwiftData database on your iPhone and stores plate inventory and related calculator settings in local app preferences. Other apps cannot access that sandbox. Use Settings → Delete All Workout Data to remove workout history while retaining your configured library. Workouts already saved to Apple Health remain there. Deleting Setvance removes its local records and preferences.

Optional iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, CloudKit can sync Setvance data across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer cannot access your private CloudKit data. You can disable iCloud access for Setvance in iPhone Settings. You can manage or delete synced Setvance data from your iCloud account-storage settings.

The hasDonated flag also uses iCloud key-value storage so its thank-you state can persist across reinstalls and your devices. It contains no payment details.

Optional Apple Health export

Apple Health export is off by default. If you enable Save completed workouts in Setvance Settings, Setvance requests write-only permission. For each workout you explicitly complete after enabling it, Setvance writes Traditional Strength Training as the type, plus the start time, end time, and duration.

Setvance does not read Apple Health data or write heart rate, calories, distance, exercises, sets, reps, or weights. The developer cannot access your Health data. Turning export off stops future saves but leaves existing Health records intact; manage permission and delete saved workouts in the Health app.

Optional in-app tips

Setvance offers three optional one-time consumable tips. Apple handles payment details through StoreKit, which displays localized prices for your App Store storefront. Setvance processes Apple's verified transaction result to complete a tip, then persists only the hasDonated true-or-false flag. It does not store payment details, receipts, or a transaction history.

For purchase or refund help, contact Apple Support. Setvance does not provide a developer-operated transaction-history or refund system.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

Setvance has no third-party analytics, advertising, social, or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not sell, rent, or share your app data with third parties. Apple Health export occurs only when you opt in and does not send Health data to the developer.

Children's privacy

Setvance is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. It has no account, messaging, or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, its effective date will be updated. Material changes will also be identified in the relevant App Store release notes.
