About Setvance

Setvance calculates sets from your training max, tracks AMRAP results, and suggests training-max changes for you to review and apply.

  • Eight built-in 5/3/1, nSuns, and GZCL programs
  • AMRAP-driven training-max suggestions
  • Plate calculator and rest timer
  • Workout history and e1RM trends
  • CSV import and export
  • Optional iCloud sync with no Setvance account

Data and privacy

Your workout data stays on your device and, if iCloud is available, can sync to your private iCloud account. Read the complete Setvance Privacy Policy.

Compatibility

Setvance requires iOS 17 or later.
